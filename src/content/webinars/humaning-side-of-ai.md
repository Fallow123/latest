Your organization bought the AI tools. People went through the training. And yet some teams use them every day while others quietly carry on as before.

That gap is rarely about the technology. It is about people: what they believe the tool means for their role, whether it feels safe to be a beginner again, and what their manager actually rewards.

In this session, applied behavioral scientist **Kip Jon** and Latest Solutions look at AI adoption from both sides: what we see in real IT rollouts, and what behavioral science says is going on underneath.

## Who should attend

- Leaders and managers responsible for rolling out AI or new platforms
- IT, HR and change teams who own adoption and training
- Anyone wondering why the tools are there but the change isn't
