Latest Solutions began in 2011 with the idea to fill the gaps we saw all too often in the IT software lifecycle. We want our customers to be confident in their investments, to keep expertise in their organizations over time, and to have friendly, professional support whenever they need it. That's why we stand behind our **White Glove Concierge Service**, across every platform we work with.

## We help businesses to:

- Implement, configure and automate their IT service, asset and endpoint management
- Deliver IT projects on time and on budget with experienced project management
- Connect systems and automate workflows with no-code tools and integrations
- Move documents to a secure, well-organized cloud DMS
- Improve the security of their systems and data
- Greatly reduce Windows 11 migration times and simplify desktop deployment
- Minimize disruption, downtime and lost revenue from technology issues or attacks
- Get people to adopt new software and AI, not just install it
- Decrease infrastructure costs by leveraging outsourced and cloud resources

## Ivanti Premier Partner

We are a proud, long-time Ivanti Premier Partner with expertise across the entire Ivanti portfolio. Our team are former Ivanti employees with 100+ years of combined Ivanti experience, bridging your business requirements and the functionality of Ivanti products.

## TeamDynamix and NetDocuments

We are certified TeamDynamix partners and work closely with them on enterprise service management, iPaaS and workflows, helping customers get full value from one no-code platform across IT, HR and operations. For legal, compliance and finance teams, we implement and support NetDocuments, giving you more control, security and organization than a SharePoint-based setup.

## Behavioral science for leadership

Technology projects succeed or fail on how people behave. Together with applied behavioral scientist Kip Jon, we run working sessions and programs that help leaders bring their teams along, and we co-host the free webinar series [Staying Human in the Age of AI](/webinars).

## Our Team

Latest Solutions is a collaboration of high-caliber consultants with a proven track record in technology, mixing experience as partners, customers and former Ivanti employees. We are certified experts in Ivanti, TeamDynamix, NetDocuments and Intercom.
