## Why we started

Latest Solutions was founded to close the gaps in the IT software lifecycle. We focus on giving customers confidence, keeping expertise with them over time, and being there when they need support. We call it our **White Glove Concierge Service**.

## We help businesses to

- Implement and automate their Ivanti investments
- Improve their cybersecurity posture
- Speed up Windows migrations
- Reduce downtime
- Simplify desktop administration
- Lower infrastructure costs
- Build new workflows to mature their use of Ivanti

## Ivanti Premier Partner

We are an Ivanti Premier Partner with expertise across the entire Ivanti portfolio. Founded in 2011, our team brings **100+ years of combined Ivanti experience**. Many of us are former Ivanti employees, so we know how to align the products with best practices.
