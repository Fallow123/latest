## FAQ

<details><summary>What is the difference between a service desk and a help desk?</summary>
A help desk fixes immediate IT problems. A service desk offers a broader catalog of IT services.
</details>
<details><summary>What are ITSM and ITIL?</summary>
ITSM is the set of practices for designing and delivering IT services. ITIL is a widely used framework for practicing ITSM.
</details>
