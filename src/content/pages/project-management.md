## Certified in the methods that matter

- **PMP (Project Management Professional):** proven discipline for scope, schedule, budget, risk and stakeholder management on projects of any size.
- **Certified Scrum Master:** agile delivery in short, visible sprints, with a backlog you can reprioritize as you learn.
- **ITIL certified:** service management best practice, so changes, releases and handoffs fit how your IT team runs day to day.

## Project management plus platform expertise

Most project managers know the method but not the tool. Ours work side by side with our Ivanti, NetDocuments and TeamDynamix engineers, so the plan reflects how the platform actually behaves. That means fewer surprises, realistic timelines and a go-live your team is ready for.

## How we run a project

1. **Discover:** understand your goals, constraints and success measures.
2. **Plan:** agree scope, milestones, roles and a communication rhythm.
3. **Deliver:** run the work in sprints or phases, with regular status reports and demos.
4. **Transition:** hand over with training, documentation and ITIL-aligned change and release.
5. **Support:** stay with you after go-live through our White Glove Concierge Service.
