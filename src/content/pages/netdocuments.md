## ndMAX: The Revolutionary Artificial Intelligence

NetDocuments’ ndMAX is an AI-powered suite designed to enhance legal workflows by automating routine tasks and providing intelligent document management solutions.

- **Legal AI Assistant:** Enables legal professionals to query documents within NetDocuments using natural language, generate summaries, timelines, and other content, all while maintaining security and permissions controls.
- **App Builder (Powered by PatternBuilder MAX):** Allows users to create scalable applications without coding, automating common legal tasks such as document drafting, data extraction, and contract analysis.
- **Intelligent Agent (ndMAX Assist):** Utilizes AI and natural language processing to help users interact with their documents more efficiently, including finding information, answering questions, creating summaries, and drafting content.

## Focusing on Security

NetDocuments is renowned for its uncompromising security, maintaining a record of zero security breaches throughout its history.

- **Advanced Encryption:** Employs multiple layers of encryption, including AES-256, to protect data both in transit and at rest.
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Enhances account security by requiring multiple forms of verification.
- **Data Loss Prevention (DLP):** Prevents unauthorized data exfiltration and mitigates risks associated with accidental or malicious data breaches.
- **Regular Security Updates:** Continuously updates its platform to address emerging threats and ensure compliance with evolving security standards.
