## What is NetDocuments?

![NetDocuments illustration](/images/netdocuments/illustration.png)

NetDocuments is a secure, cloud-based vault for managing critical documents. It is the central source of truth for law firms, legal teams and financial institutions, supporting access, organization and compliance.

## ndMAX: AI for legal work

ndMAX is an AI suite that automates routine legal tasks and improves document management.

- **Legal AI Assistant:** ask questions of your documents in plain language and generate summaries and timelines, while keeping existing permissions and security controls.
- **App Builder (powered by PatternBuilder MAX):** build applications for drafting, data extraction and contract analysis without coding.
- **Intelligent Agent (ndMAX Assist):** find information, answer questions, summarize and draft content.

## Focused on security

- Multi-layer encryption, including AES-256, for data in transit and at rest
- Multi-factor authentication
- Data loss prevention against unauthorized exfiltration
- Regular platform security updates
