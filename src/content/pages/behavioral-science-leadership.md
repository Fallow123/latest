## What we help with

- **AI tools that aren't being used.** The licenses are paid for and the training is done, but adoption is patchy.
- **Training that doesn't change behavior.** People nod along, then go back to the old way.
- **Managers who have become bottlenecks.** Decisions, approvals and knowledge all route through a few people.
- **New or merged teams that have to deliver before they gel.**
- **Trust that broke after a restructure, layoffs or a difficult change.**

## How we work

What people say is happening, what we see happening, and what is actually driving the behavior are often three different things. We start by looking beneath the behavior: what people believe, what feels risky, and what the system really rewards.

Then we work with your leaders on a small number of specific changes, and check that they hold. Latest Solutions brings two decades of experience rolling out technology in real organizations; [Kip Jon](https://www.kipjon.com) brings more than 20 years as an applied behavioral scientist and executive coach.
