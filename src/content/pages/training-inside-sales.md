## Who it's for

- **Career changers** from retail, hospitality, customer service, teaching or the military.
- **Recent graduates** who want a fast route into the tech industry.
- **New sales reps** in their first tech role who want to ramp up faster.

## Why inside sales?

Tech companies are always hiring people who can start conversations and build relationships. Inside sales roles often come with base pay plus commission, clear targets and a path into account executive, customer success or sales leadership roles.
