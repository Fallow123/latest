Questions

Before you enroll

Is any of it in person?

No. The consultation, all live classes, coaching, demo day and graduation happen on Zoom. You need a computer, a headset and a reliable internet connection.

Do I need sales experience?

Not for SDR Academy or AI Basic Knowledge. The Hunting Playbook is built for working SDRs and AEs who already prospect.

How much time does it take each week?

SDR Academy is three 45-minute live sessions plus about 2–3 hours of assignments, so plan on 4–5 hours a week for 6 weeks.

What if I miss a session?

Every session is recorded. To graduate you attend live or watch the recording for every session and submit every deliverable.

Do I have to pay for the software?

No. You use free versions of HubSpot, Salesforce Trailhead and Apollo. Paid tools such as ZoomInfo and Gong are taught through guided walkthroughs.

Do you guarantee a job?

No program can honestly guarantee a job. You get the skills, materials and practice hiring managers look for, plus demo day and the alumni job board.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Each course has its own Certificate of Completion with a unique ID, issued when you finish the requirements listed on that course page. It shows you completed the training. It is not an accredited degree or a licensed industry certification.

Which course should I take first?

New to sales: SDR Academy, then AI Basic Knowledge. Already prospecting: go straight to the Hunting Playbook and add AI Basic Knowledge if you are not using AI daily.

The house rules

Three rules we teach in every lesson

Verify before you send

AI drafts, you decide. Every fact in an email or brief gets checked against a source you can name.

Protect customer data

No customer names, contracts or personal details in tools your company hasn't approved.

Keep your voice

Buyers reply to people. Use AI to get to a first draft faster, then make it sound like you.

Module 3 · Partners and contract vehicles

Get buyable before you get a meeting

Public buyers move fastest when they can purchase through a contract they already trust. Find out which partner already knows the account, get the intro or the intel, and lead with the vehicle.

  • Carahsoft
  • NASPO ValuePoint
  • Sourcewell
  • OMNIA Partners
  • TIPS-USA
  • E&I Cooperative
  • State IT contracts
  • GSA Cooperative Purchasing

Partner checklist

  • List every contract your product is on, with numbers and expiry dates
  • Map each Top 10 and Tier 1 account to the vehicle it can buy through
  • Register every deal to protect pricing and reseller margin
  • Request vehicle pricing early so procurement can move without an RFP
  • Watch contract expirations as re-bid windows
  • Confirm piggyback rights before relying on a state or co-op contract

Module 4 · Account plans and MEDDPICC

Know what you have, and what you still need

Every Top 10, Tier 1 and Top Priority account gets five artifacts: an account plan, a MEDDPICC score, an org chart, a relationship map and a budget map. Score each element 0 (need), 1 (partial) or 2 (have), and work the biggest gap first.

M

Metrics

The measurable outcome they care about: backlog days, request turnaround, staff hours, audit findings closed.

E

Economic buyer

Who can release funds, and above what dollar threshold it goes to council, board or the state CIO.

D

Decision criteria

Security certifications, integrations, references, price and contract vehicle.

D

Decision process

Department, IT governance, security, legal, procurement, then council or board, in that order.

P

Paper process

Which vehicle, the PO steps, legal review, agenda deadlines and who signs.

I

Identify pain

What's broken, who feels it, and what happens if nothing changes.

C

Champion

Someone with power who is selling for you internally, and who you have tested.

C

Competition

The incumbent, other vendors, an in-house build, or doing nothing.

Follow the money. Every plan names the funding source the budget owner will actually charge: general fund, capital project fund, enterprise fund, federal or state grant, or a shared state contract. Then it names the fiscal year-end deadline for issuing a PO.

Module 5 · Cadences and messaging

Flows built per tier, messages built per persona

Top 10 · Executive

Ongoing. Exec-to-exec note on day 2, multi-thread 4–6 stakeholders with personal video, partner co-sell, and an onsite workshop tied to their budget cycle. Never quiet for more than 5 business days.

Tier 1 · Strategic

21 days, 12 touches. Initiative-specific email, voicemail, LinkedIn, a 60–90 second video on their public plan, partner intro, peer case study, event invite, and a pivot to nurture.

Tier 2 and 3

Tier 2 runs 6 touches over 14 days with a "buyable via cooperative contract" proof point. Tier 3 is a 30-day automated nurture that promotes on intent.
